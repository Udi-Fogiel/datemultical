Date Field Symbols Reference (UTS #35 Date Field Symbol Table)
==============================================================

Unicode UTS #35, LDML Part 4: Dates
Reference:
https://unicode.org/reports/tr35/tr35-dates.html

Pattern field length generally controls the field's formatting style.
For numeric fields, the length normally specifies the minimum number
of digits, with zero-padding as necessary.

Typical field lengths:
  1 | Numeric, 1–2 digits       | M, d, H, etc.
  2 | Numeric, 2 digits         | MM, dd, HH, etc.
  3 | Abbreviated               | MMM, EEE
  4 | Wide / full               | MMMM, EEEE
  5 | Narrow                    | MMMMM, EEEEE

For some fields, additional lengths have special meanings.

'l' is deprecated and no longer used.


Era - G
-------
G..GGG  | Abbreviated era name.      G=AD
GGGG    | Wide era name.              GGGG=Anno Domini
GGGGG   | Narrow era name.            GGGGG=A


Year - y Y u U r
----------------
y       | Calendar year.
yy      | Two low-order year digits, zero-padded.
yyyy    | Calendar year, minimum 4 digits.
        | Example: y=2024, yy=24, yyyy=2024.

Y       | Week-based year.
YY      | Two low-order week-based year digits.
YYYY    | Week-based year, minimum 4 digits.
        | Used with week-of-year (w), especially for ISO week dates.

u       | Extended year, without an era.
        | All field lengths specify a minimum number of digits.
        | Year 0 is allowed.

U..UUU  | Cyclic year name, abbreviated.
UUUU    | Cyclic year name, wide.
UUUUU   | Cyclic year name, narrow.
        | Used by calendars such as the Chinese calendar.

r       | Related Gregorian year.
        | Mainly relevant for non-Gregorian calendars.


Quarter - Q q
-------------
Q       | Quarter, numeric or abbreviated.
QQ      | Quarter, two digits.
QQQ     | Abbreviated quarter name.      Q3
QQQQ    | Wide quarter name.             3rd quarter
QQQQQ   | Narrow quarter name.

q       | Stand-alone quarter.
        | Same width distinctions as Q, but used in stand-alone context.


Month - M L
-----------
M       | Month in format context.
        | M=9, MM=09, MMM=Sep, MMMM=September, MMMMM=S

L       | Month in stand-alone context.
        | Same width distinctions as M, but used when the month
        | is displayed independently.


Week / Day - w W d D F g
------------------------
w       | Week of year.
ww      | Week of year, two digits.

W       | Week of month.

d       | Day of month.
dd      | Day of month, two digits.
        | Example: d=1, dd=01

D       | Day of year.
DDD     | Day of year, three digits.

F       | Day of week in month.
        | Example: F=1 means the first occurrence of that weekday
        | within the month.

g       | Modified Julian Day.


Weekday - E e c
---------------
E..EEE  | Abbreviated weekday name.
        | Example: E=Tue, EEE=Tue

EEEE    | Wide / full weekday name.
        | Example: Tuesday

EEEEE   | Narrow weekday name.
        | Example: T

EEEEEE  | Short weekday name.
        | Example: Tu

e       | Local weekday.
        | e=2, ee=02 are numeric weekday forms.
        | eee=Tue, eeee=Tuesday

c       | Stand-alone local weekday.
        | Used for weekday names in stand-alone context.


Day Period - a b B
------------------
a       | AM/PM.
        | Example: PM

b       | AM/PM, including special forms such as midnight and noon.

B       | Flexible day period.
        | Examples: morning, afternoon, evening, night.


Hour - h H K k j J C
--------------------
h       | Hour in 12-hour cycle, 1–12.
hh      | Two-digit hour.

H       | Hour in 24-hour cycle, 0–23.
HH      | Two-digit hour.

K       | Hour in 12-hour cycle, 0–11.
KK      | Two-digit hour.

k       | Hour in 24-hour cycle, 1–24.
kk      | Two-digit hour.

j       | Locale-preferred hour cycle.
        | Selects the locale's preferred hour symbol.

J       | Locale-preferred hour without a day-period field.

C       | Locale-dependent hour-cycle representation.


Minute / Second / Fraction - m s S A
------------------------------------
m       | Minute.
mm      | Two-digit minute.

s       | Second.
ss      | Two-digit second.

S       | Fractional second.
SS      | Hundredths of a second.
SSS     | Thousandths of a second.
        | Example: S=.1, SS=.12, SSS=.123

A       | Milliseconds in day.


Time Separator
--------------
:
        | Literal time separator in the pattern.
        | Its rendered form may be localized.


Time Zone - z Z O v V
---------------------
z..zzz  | Short time-zone name.
        | Example: PDT

zzzz    | Long time-zone name.
        | Example: Pacific Daylight Time

Z..ZZZ  | RFC 822 / ISO-style offset.
        | Z=-0800

ZZZZ    | Localized GMT format.
        | Example: GMT-08:00

ZZZZZ   | ISO 8601 extended offset with Z for UTC.
        | Example: -08:00 or Z

O       | Short localized GMT format.
        | Example: GMT-8

OOOO    | Long localized GMT format.
        | Example: GMT-08:00

v       | Short generic time-zone name.
        | Example: PT

vvvv    | Long generic time-zone name.
        | Example: Pacific Time

V       | Short time-zone ID.
VV      | Time-zone ID.
        | Example: America/Los_Angeles

VVV     | Time-zone exemplar city.
        | Example: Los Angeles

VVVV    | Generic location format.


ISO 8601 Offset - X x
---------------------
X       | ISO 8601 offset; uses Z for zero offset.
XX      | Basic format: -0800
XXX     | Extended format: -08:00

XXXX    | Basic format with optional seconds.
XXXXX   | Extended format with optional seconds.

x       | ISO 8601 offset; uses +0000 instead of Z.
xx      | Basic format: +0000
xxx     | Extended format: +00:00

xxxx    | Basic format with optional seconds.
xxxxx   | Extended format with optional seconds.


Combined Pattern Examples
-------------------------
Pattern: yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm:ss
Output:  2025-09-08 09:08:08

Pattern: yyyy-MM-dd'T'HH:mm:ssXXX
Output:  2025-09-08T09:08:08+03:30
